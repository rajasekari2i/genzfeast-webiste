How it works

From craving to counter in four taps

No standing in line during a 15-minute break. Order from class, collect when you get there.

  1. Browse the menu

    See today's items from your canteen with prices, photos and clear veg or non-veg marks. Sold-out items are flagged.

  2. Add to cart

    Pick what you want and how many. The cart keeps the running total.

  3. Pay with UPI

    Checkout through Razorpay using any UPI app. If a payment fails, tap Pay again on the same order.

  4. Show your code

    Once payment goes through you get a 6-digit pickup code. Read it out at the counter and collect your food.

About us

Lunch breaks are short. Queues shouldn't eat them.

GenzFeast connects college canteens with the students and staff they feed.

Why we built it

On most campuses the canteen is busiest in the same fifteen minutes every day. Students spend the break in line, canteen staff juggle cash and change, and popular items run out while people are still waiting to ask for them.

GenzFeast moves the ordering and paying out of the queue. Students order from their phones, pay with UPI, and walk up with a pickup code. The counter only has to check the code and hand over the food.

Built for each canteen

Every canteen on GenzFeast runs as its own space, with its own menu, staff, students and orders, and its own branded app. One canteen can never see another's data.

Security

How we protect your account, your payments and your canteen's data.

Each canteen is walled off

Every canteen's menu, users, orders and feedback are kept separate. This separation is checked twice: once by our application, and again by the database itself, so a mistake in one layer is not enough to expose another canteen's data.

Your account

  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, a modern algorithm designed to resist cracking. Nobody at GenzFeast or your canteen can read your password.
  • Short-lived sign-in. Your session uses tokens that expire after 30 minutes and are renewed securely in the background. Signing out ends that renewal.
  • Lock after repeated failures. After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked. Resetting your password with a one-time code unlocks it.
  • One-time reset codes are sent by SMS or push notification and are stored only in hashed form.

Your payments

  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. GenzFeast never sees your UPI PIN or bank details.
  • An order is marked paid only when Razorpay confirms it to our server with a signed message. A payment screen on the phone alone cannot mark an order as paid.
  • Prices are always taken from the canteen's menu on our server, never from the app.

Your pickup code

  • The 6-digit code is created once your payment succeeds and is sent only to you.
  • Counter staff cannot look it up. They type in the code you read out and the app tells them whether it matches.
  • Each code works once. Every attempt, right or wrong, is logged.

Records we keep

Sign-ins, account locks, order changes and payment events are recorded so problems can be traced. Change history for menu items, orders, payments and feedback is written by the database itself and cannot be edited by the app.

Help & Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Contact details are at the bottom.

Account

How do I get the app?

Each canteen has its own GenzFeast app. Ask your canteen for the download link for your campus.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your username. You'll get a one-time code by SMS or as a notification, depending on your canteen. Enter the code and choose a new password.

My account is locked

Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Use Forgot password to reset your password; that unlocks your account. There's no automatic unlock after waiting.

Can teaching or non-teaching staff order?

Not yet. Staff accounts can sign in, update their profile, contact the canteen and send feedback. Ordering is currently available to students.

Orders and payment

My payment failed. What now?

Open the order in My Orders and tap Pay again. You'll pay for the same order, so you won't end up with a duplicate.

Money left my account but the order says payment failed

UPI confirmations can take a few minutes. Check My Orders again shortly. If it still shows failed, contact your canteen with the order details.

Why can't I place a new order?

You can only have one unpaid order at a time. Finish paying for it in My Orders first.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders can't be cancelled from the app. Talk to your canteen staff.

Pickup

Where is my pickup code?

It's sent as a notification when your payment succeeds, and it's always shown on the order in My Orders until you collect your food.

The counter says my code is wrong

Check that you're reading the code for the right order in My Orders. Each code works only once, so a delivered order's code won't work again.

For canteens

How do we join GenzFeast?

Contact us below. We set up your canteen, create your first manager account and prepare your branded app. Your manager can then add staff, students and the menu.

How do staff mark an item sold out?

In the Products screen, flip the switch next to the item. Students see it as sold out straight away.
